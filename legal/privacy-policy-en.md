# Privacy Policy - Sarix Go

**Last updated:** May 29, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Sarix Go ("we", "us", "our app") is a mobile application for Termiz Sariosiyo taxi service. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

By using the app, you agree to this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you provide
- **Phone number** — for registration and verification
- **Name** — to display to drivers
- **Language preference** — for app interface
- **Order details** — pickup, destination, time, notes

### 2.2 Automatically collected
- **Location (GPS)** — only with your permission, for automatic address detection
- **Device identifier** — for technical error analysis
- **App usage statistics** — to improve the app

### 2.3 We DO NOT collect
- ❌ Bank card information (payment is direct to driver)
- ❌ Photos, videos, or personal files
- ❌ Contact lists
- ❌ SMS messages or call logs

## 3. How We Use Information

Collected data is used to:
- Provide taxi services (find driver, notify you)
- Verify your phone number (via SMS or Telegram)
- Deliver your order to drivers
- Ensure app security
- Customer service and technical support
- Bonuses and promo codes

## 4. Sharing with Third Parties

We share your information with the following service providers:

- **Yandex Maps** — for map display and address detection (per Yandex privacy policy)
- **Telegram** — for verification codes via bot
- **SMS provider (Eskiz.uz)** — for SMS sending (phone number only)
- **Drivers** — your name, phone, and address to fulfill orders

We do **NOT sell** your data for advertising and do **NOT give** it to third parties for marketing.

## 5. Data Storage

- Data is stored on servers in Uzbekistan or secure international servers
- Order history is kept for up to 2 years
- Phone number is stored while account is active
- If you delete your account, data is removed within 30 days (except as required by law)

## 6. Data Security

We implement the following security measures:
- 🔒 HTTPS encryption
- 🔐 JWT authentication
- 🛡 Passwords stored in SecureStore
- 👁 Only verified drivers can access sensitive data

No system is 100% secure, but we take all reasonable measures to protect your data.

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- ✅ **View your data** — in profile
- ✅ **Edit** — name, language
- ✅ **Delete** — request account deletion via admin
- ✅ **Export history** — upon request
- ✅ **Withdraw consent** — by stopping app use

## 8. Children

This app is intended for users **18 and older**. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18.

## 9. Permissions

The app requests:
- **Location** — for address detection (only while app is open)
- **Internet** — for server communication
- **Notifications** — for new messages
- **Vibration** — for alerts

You can disable each in your device settings.

## 10. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes through the app.
