## Full description (4000 chars max)
```
🚕 SARIX GO - Termiz Sariosiyo Taxi Service

Reliable and convenient taxi between districts of Surxondaryo region, Uzbekistan. Order a driver with one tap and arrive at your destination quickly.

✨ WHY CHOOSE US?

🗺️ AVAILABLE ROUTES
• Termiz ↔ Sariosiyo
• Termiz ↔ Uzun
• Termiz ↔ Denov
• Termiz ↔ Sho'rchi
• Sariosiyo ↔ Jarqo'rg'on
• Sariosiyo ↔ Qumqo'rg'on
• And other Surxondaryo districts

💰 CLEAR PRICES
Set prices for each route, but you can negotiate with the driver. No hidden fees!

📦 PARCEL SERVICE
Send documents and packages to another city quickly - starting from 30,000 UZS.

🚗 WHOLE CAR
Book the entire car - 4 seats at your disposal.

⚡ QUICK CONNECTION
As soon as you place an order - a driver will be found. Track in real-time.

📱 EASY TO USE
• Telegram-assisted phone verification by sharing your own contact
• 4 languages: Uzbek (Latin/Cyrillic), Russian, English
• Yandex Maps integration
• Cash payment for trips

🎁 BONUS SYSTEM
Invite friends and earn points. Get discounts with promo codes!

🔒 SAFE
• Phone ownership verified through Telegram contact sharing
• Driver information visible
• Only approved drivers can receive orders
• Female-only filter available

📞 AVAILABLE 24/7
Order anytime - day or night.

💼 FOR WHOM?
• Residents of Termiz, Sariosiyo, Uzun, Denov, Sho'rchi, Jarqo'rg'on, Qumqo'rg'on
• Frequent inter-district travelers
• Students and workers
• Document senders

📲 HOW IT WORKS?
1. Start verification from the app
2. Open Telegram and share your own phone contact with the bot
3. Return to the app after verification
4. Choose your departure, destination, tariff, and passenger count
5. Place the order and wait for an approved driver

📞 Contact
Telegram: @termizsariosiyotaxi_bot
Region: Surxondaryo, Uzbekistan

With Sarix Go, every journey is easy and safe!
```
