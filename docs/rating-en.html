Rating system

Sarix Go - Termiz Sariosiyo Taxi

Last updated: 28 August 2026

O'zbekcha · Русский · English

In short

After every completed ride the passenger rates the driver and the driver rates the passenger, from 1 to 5 stars. A rating is the plain average of every star received.

1. Who rates whom

Rating goes both ways:

  • Passenger → driver. Once the ride is completed, the rating screen opens in the passenger app.
  • Driver → passenger. When the driver completes the ride, they are shown the same screen.

Both are optional. You can skip with "Later" — skipping has no effect on anyone's rating.

2. What can be rated

  • Only a completed ride can be rated.
  • A cancelled order cannot be rated.
  • An order that found no driver (expired) cannot be rated.
  • Each side rates a given ride once.
  • A submitted rating cannot be changed or deleted.

3. What the stars mean

StarsMeaning
1Very bad
2Bad
3Average
4Good
5Excellent

Only whole stars are accepted — there is no such value as 4.5.

4. Comment

You may add a comment alongside the stars, but that is optional too. Length is up to 500 characters.

5. How the rating is calculated

A rating is the plain average of every star received. There is no time weighting and no dropping of older ratings: the first one counts exactly as much as the latest.

For example: 5, 4, 5, 5 — the average is 4.75, displayed as 4.8.

It is shown to one decimal place, with how many ratings it is based on next to it — for example "Rating · 12 ratings". That matters: 5.0 from 1 rating and 4.8 from 200 are entirely different things.

6. When there are no ratings yet

If nobody has rated you, the number is replaced by "—" and the words "No ratings yet".

We deliberately do not show an invented starting value. A new driver sitting at "5.0" creates false confidence, and "4.0" bears no relation to reality at all. The number appears only once there is a real rating behind it.

7. Where the rating is visible

  • A driver sees their own rating on their profile and under "Statistics".
  • A passenger sees the driver's rating in the driver's details.

8. What a rating affects

At present a rating does not affect order distribution: an order goes out to every eligible driver at the same time, and the ride goes to whoever accepts first. Ratings exist for transparency and quality control.

A persistently low rating together with complaints is reviewed by an administrator and may lead to the account being blocked (clause 7 of the Terms of Service).
